From scenario to skill in three simple steps.

We designed The Reflection so that 92% of users can start without training. L&D teams stay in control of content, language and competencies while learners just press play.

Step 1
Select or create a scenario

Choose from a growing library or generate a custom scenario with our AI builder. Align every simulation to a specific skill, role and difficulty level.

Step 2
Practice with an AI partner

Learners join a short, focused role-play with an AI client, teammate or customer. Sessions last 5–20 minutes and fit naturally into a busy workday.

Step 3
Review instant feedback

Right on the video timeline, learners see what they did well, where they lost trust, and how to improve on the next attempt—turning reflection into measurable progress.
